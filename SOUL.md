# LUXON Product Soul

LUXON exists to make investment judgment auditable.

The core product promise is simple: every number on the screen can answer where
it came from, when it became knowable, how it was transformed, and whether it is
safe to use.

## Principles

- **Evidence before display**: no source trace, no production number.
- **Time matters**: calculations must respect what was knowable at the selected
  point in time.
- **Forecasts are assumptions**: consensus, user input, deterministic models,
  and AI commentary are separate layers.
- **A chart is an argument**: price, fundamentals, fair value, normal multiple,
  dividends, and forecast bands should make the valuation thesis inspectable.
- **AI supports judgment**: AI can summarize and critique, but it cannot invent
  financial values or replace the source ledger.

## Design Posture

The interface should feel like a compact research workspace: fast search,
source-aware charts, clear controls, dense but readable tables, and a visible
audit rail. It should avoid decorative complexity and keep the user's attention
on evidence, assumptions, and valuation consequences.

## Product Boundary

LUXON is not a broker, not a signal service, and not a copy of a commercial
terminal. It is the productized underwriting terminal in the FUND Stack: the
place where public filings, market data, forecasts, and judgment criteria meet
inside a source-audited research workflow.

The first users may be the operator, invited collaborators, or a small research
group. That does not make the product a personal utility. It should be built as
an early-access product whose access, data licensing, tenant scoping, and audit
exports can mature without weakening the source-trace contract.
